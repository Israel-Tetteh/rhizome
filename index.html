Rhizome

See the literature around any paper.

Rhizome maps the papers most similar to the one you start from, using shared references (bibliographic coupling) and shared citers (co-citation). Papers that sit close together are closely related, even when they never cite each other.

  • Free and open source. No account, no paywall, MIT licence.
  • Open data. Built on OpenAlex, 250M+ works, CC0.
  • Fits your reference manager. Export any list to BibTeX, RIS or CSV for Zotero, Mendeley or EndNote.
