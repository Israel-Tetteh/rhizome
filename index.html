      Start from several papers

      Rhizome finds the papers most related to the whole set, so a handful of papers you already know gives a sharper map of your topic than any single one.

      In Zotero: select papers, right click, Export Items, then choose BibTeX or RIS. Entries without a DOI are skipped. Up to 25 papers.

      Settings

      OpenAlex is free. Without a key, each network gets a small daily budget, enough for dozens of graphs. A free key gives you ten times more. It is stored only in this browser.
